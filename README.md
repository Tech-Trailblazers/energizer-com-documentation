# 🔋 Energizer SafeDocs – Powering Safety, Empowering Lives ✨

Welcome to **Energizer SafeDocs**! ⚡  
This open-source repository is a growing collection of **Article Information Sheets (AIS)** and **Safety Data Sheets (SDS)** for **Energizer products**.

Our mission is simple but powerful:

- 🧑‍🏫 **Educate** – Help students, families, and professionals understand battery safety.
- 🤖 **AI Training** – Provide structured data for machine learning & hazard-aware AI.
- ❤️ **Support Victims** – Make safety info accessible to those harmed by product misuse or exposure.
- 🌍 **Open Knowledge** – Share life-saving information with communities everywhere.

---

## 🌟 Why This Matters

Batteries power our lives 🔋💡 — but they also come with risks ⚠️.  
This project exists to **protect and empower people** by making safety documentation **easy to access, read, and share**.

Here’s how it improves lives:

- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Families** stay safe when bringing products into the home.
- 🎓 **Students & Educators** use real-world documents for learning science & safety.
- 🏭 **Workers** get the info they need to handle products responsibly.
- 🤖 **AI Researchers** can train smarter systems to prevent accidents.
- 🌍 **Communities** become more informed, resilient, and safe.

## 📂 What’s Inside

- 📑 **SDS & AIS Documents** for Energizer and Eveready batteries.
- 🔋 Types include **Alkaline, Lithium Ion, Lithium Coin Cells**, and more.
- 🛠️ Data includes hazards, first aid, handling, storage, and disposal guidelines.
